# Changelog

## 0.2.3 — 20 November 2026

- **Send feedback** from Settings: report a bug, request a feature, send a message or rate
  Hanami, on the Vythera feedback page with your version and phone already filled in.
- After a crash, the log is saved to Download/Hanami so you can attach it to a bug report.
  **Save crash logs** in Settings keeps one for every crash.
- An anonymous check-in counts the phones using Hanami (see the privacy policy).

## 0.2.2 — 18 November 2026

- The GitHub build checks for new stable releases when it opens and from Settings.
- Welcome walkthrough with tips in Explore, Library and on title pages.
- Share Hanami with your own message, and see what's new from Settings.

## 0.2.1 — 18 November 2026

- Trakt sync moves to Hanami's production Trakt app. If you connected in 0.2.0, reconnect once.

## 0.2.0 — 17 November 2026

- **Trakt sync.** Connect with a short code; your Trakt history, watchlist and scores
  come into Hanami, and everything you mark watched goes back to Trakt.
- **Your numbers, expanded.** Daily activity heatmap, streaks, the year so far, month by
  month, when you watch most and your most-watched titles.
- Hanami now remembers when you watched each episode or film.

## 0.1.0 — 16 November 2026

- First test build: watch list, episode progress, Explore, Schedule, reminders and
  release alerts, sharing to Hanami, optional episode tracking from other apps.
