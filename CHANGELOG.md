# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 17 November 2026

- **Trakt sync.** Connect with a short code; your Trakt history, watchlist and scores
  come into Hanami, and everything you mark watched goes back to Trakt.
- **Your numbers, expanded.** Daily activity heatmap, streaks, the year so far, month by
  month, when you watch most and your most-watched titles.
- Hanami now remembers when you watched each episode or film.

## 0.1.0 — 16 November 2026

- First test build: watch list, episode progress, Explore, Schedule, reminders and
  release alerts, sharing to Hanami, optional episode tracking from other apps.
